# Rites and the caern

Rites are the Garou's ceremonies. They mark rank, bind packs and spirits, cleanse taint, honour
the dead, punish the guilty, and open and keep the caern.

## Performing a rite

1. Select the Garou who will lead it, the ritemaster, and press **Perform a rite**.
2. Pick the rite, then its subject or place if it asks for one.
3. The colony's Garou on the map come to join the circle. Kinfolk come too for Caern Building.
4. When the rite ends, everyone within 6 cells of the ritemaster is the circle.

A rite takes half an hour a level. **Call off the rite** sends the circle back to work, and the
rite isn't performed.

- **The roll** depends on the rite's type, as at the table. Every five Garou past the rite's
  minimum take one off the difficulty, never below 3. The Master of the Rite rolls a die more at
  home.
- **Too few:** a ring on the ground marks the circle while a rite runs, and only those inside it count. If the time is up with fewer Garou in the ring than the rite needs, the ritemaster says so and waits until enough arrive. Give the ritemaster another order to call it off. While a rite runs, no one in the circle falls asleep or starves: they get very tired and urgently hungry, and no worse.
- **Failure** wastes the time. **A botch** offends the spirits and costs the ritemaster two
  Wisdom.
- Rites cost no experience.

## Rituals and learning

**Rituals** is the lore of the rites, on the Garou tab's Rites page: a dot for every two rites
known, one more for Theurges and Philodox. A rite's level can't be higher than the Garou's
Rituals.

A new Cliath knows one random level 1 rite, a Theurge two. A cub gets theirs at the Rite of
Passage. To learn more, press **Learn a rite...** on the Rites page:

- from a Garou here who knows it: two lessons a level, one a day, half as many for a Theurge;
- from a lune in the Penumbra, for its chiminage. See [The Umbra and spirits](The-Umbra-and-Spirits.md).

## Rites on their own

- **The Moot Rite** happens at each full moon when the colony has a caern and two Garou, as a
  gathering like a party. At the moot:
  - the colony Garou with the most Renown since the last moot, at least 3, is hailed: a point of
    Glory and a good mood;
  - any who broke the Litany since the last moot and haven't done the Rite of Contrition are
    judged: a point of Honor lost and a bad mood;
  - a colony Galliard tells the best tale since the last moot of a colonist's capture, kill or death.
    Its hero gains a point of Glory if Garou, the teller a point of Wisdom, and everyone there is
    cheered.
- **Gathering for the Departed** happens after a colonist dies, as a funeral.
- A Garou who knows a **minor rite** performs it in free time as recreation, once a day, for its
  blessing.

You can call off any of these.

### Punishment rites

These are led on a Garou of the colony who broke a tenet of the Litany in the last season. One
called on a Garou who hasn't fails, and the ritemaster loses 5 Honor for it.

| Rite | Level | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Rite of the Jackdaw | 1 | The offender blurts things out and insults people, a day a success |
| Rite of Ostracism | 2 | Costs Renown, and packmates think less of the offender until the next moon phase |
| Stone of Scorn | 2 | Costs 8 Honor and 2 Wisdom |
| Voice of the Jackal | 2 | Costs Renown, and the offender's social skills drop until the ritemaster lifts it |
| The Hunt | 3 | The offender is cast out and runs for the map edge. Bringing them down pays the hunters 2 Honor |
| Satire Rite | 3 | At the caern, against the offender's rank plus 4. They lose a rank. A botch costs the ritemaster 5 Wisdom |

## The caern

A caern is a place of power the Garou have woken. The colony can have one.

### Opening one

1. **Mark a place of power.** It's in the Misc build menu. It must be outdoors, on wild natural
   ground, with no more than two colony buildings within 15 cells.
2. **Cleanse it.** Perform the Rite of Cleansing there.
3. **Perform the Rite of Caern Building** at it. It must begin at dusk, between 17:00 and 22:00,
   under a waxing moon (days 2 to 5 of the quadrum), and it runs through the night.
   - It needs three Garou and 10 Gnosis in the circle. Kinfolk give a point each, and every Garou
     gives up a point of permanent Gnosis.
   - The Wyrm feels it. Its servants come before the night is out: keep them away from the
     circle.
   - Wits plus Rituals at 8. The successes set the level: 1 to 3 make level 1, 4 to 6 level 2, 7
     and 8 level 3, 9 or more level 4.
   - Failure burns the circle.

The land around it sets the caern's type: water, geysers, trees, rock, hills or ruins. The rite
also picks the caern's totem, who shows itself at moots.

### What it does

- Garou within 12 cells get back a quarter of a Gnosis a level, every so often.
- The Gauntlet is thin there: easier to [step sideways](The-Umbra-and-Spirits.md#stepping-sideways),
  and a Garou with no Gnosis left can still cross back at the caern.
- Every three moots held at a level raise it by one, up to 5.
- The **Rite of the Opened Caern** gives by type: Gnosis, Rage, Willpower, healing or a view of
  the land at once, or for the other types a blessing that lasts half a day a level.

| Type | The Opened Caern gives |
|---|---|
| Enigmas | Faster research |
| Gnosis | Gnosis for the ritemaster |
| Healing | The ritemaster's wounds closed |
| Leadership | Words that carry more weight |
| Rage | Rage for the ritemaster |
| Stamina | Blows land softer |
| Strength | Blows land harder |
| The City | Better trades |
| Visions | The land around it shown |
| Will | Willpower back |
| Wisdom | Everything learned faster |
| The Wyld | Something different every time |

### Losing it

The Wyrm wants the caern. About half its raids go for the circle first. Enemies who hold the
circle for three hours with no colony Garou in it cost a level, and at 0 the caern dies and needs
Caern Building again. Losing the caern sends every Garou of the colony toward Harano.

**Spirit poachers**: 3 to 5 trappers of an enemy faction come to hold the caern and bottle its
spirits. While they hold it, it loses levels as when it's overrun. Each one killed or downed breaks
its traps: the colony's Garou on the map get their Gnosis back, and the one who did it gains a
point of Honor. Left alone, they leave after half a day.

### The Opened Bridge

The Rite of the Opened Bridge needs a pathstone, carried or lying near the ritemaster. One lies on
about one Penumbra map in ten, guarded by a spirit, and a sept sometimes pays one for a mission.
It needs a sept that isn't hostile, and rolls Wits plus Enigmas at 8 less the
caern's level, three dice more when the pack's totem is the caern's.

The bridge runs for a year from the caern to that sept. **Cross the bridge**, on the caern, sends
everyone standing in the circle to the sept. **Cross the bridge home**, on the caravan, brings
them back. While it holds, the sept comes at once when the Wyrm attacks. A moot renews it.
