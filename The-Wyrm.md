# The Wyrm

The Wyrm is corruption, decay and destruction gone mad, and the Garou's oldest enemy. Its
servants come for colonies with Garou. The setting "The Wyrm attacks" turns all of it off.

## Wyrm taint

Taint lies on the ground as well as in creatures. Select a Garou to see it on the map.

- Each hour a few tainted cells spread onto their neighbours, more the bigger the patch, up to 600
  cells. Plants on a few of them wither.
- A fomor or a bane-ridden beast that dies spills a small patch where it falls.
- A Garou within 8 cells of taint gains half a point of Rage each hour.
- A Garou near taint can be [pulled into the Penumbra](The-Umbra-and-Spirits.md#pulled-across).
- **The Rite of Cleansing**, aimed at the ground, lifts it in a circle, wider with each success.
  The Rite of the Opened Sky clears it under its rain.

Taint in a Garou comes from the [Thrall of the Wyrm](Frenzy-and-the-Litany.md#the-thrall-of-the-wyrm).
Sense Wyrm finds the tainted, the Wyrm's servants and, with Kindred loaded, some vampires.

## The Wyrm's servants

| Enemy | What they are |
|---|---|
| Fomori | People possessed by banes and twisted. Each has one or more powers, and the Delirium doesn't touch them |
| Banes | The Wyrm's spirits. In the Penumbra they attack whoever comes near. On the home map one rides the biggest wild animal it can find, or walks in in its own shape. Its blow can get inside a mortal colonist (see [Possession](#possession)) |
| Black Spiral Dancers | Fallen Garou, a fourteenth tribe. They fight in Crinos with claws, frenzy and Gifts, and raid by night. A colony Garou lost to the Thrall becomes one |
| Pentex | A corporation that serves the Wyrm. It opens plants near the colony |
| First Teams | Pentex mercenaries: gunners, blades with silver, and a few fomori |

### Fomori powers

Every fomor has Armored Skin or Berserker, and often one or two more:

| Power | What it does |
|---|---|
| Armored Skin | A quarter less damage from everything |
| Armored Hide, Cancerous Carapace | Hide or growths that blades slide off. Not both on one fomor |
| Exoskeleton | A bony shell: stronger and harder to hurt |
| Berserker | Hits harder and faster, and a wound can send it into a frenzy |
| Body-Barbs | Blades of bone from its joints |
| Claws and Fangs | Wounds like a werewolf's, aggravated |
| Darksight | Sees as well in the dark as in daylight |
| Extra Limbs | Extra arms or tendrils to strike with |
| Noxious Breath | Fumes that burn the lungs of anyone close |

Some have a power of the mind as well:

| Power | What it does |
|---|---|
| Cause Insanity | A colonist it can see within 15 cells goes psychotic for two hours, unless they make Willpower at 7 |
| Echoes of Wrath | A colonist it can see goes berserk for a minute, unless they make Willpower at 7 |
| Animal Control | Once a fight, the wild animals within 30 cells join its side and its raid |
| Brain Eating | Its blows gnaw at the mind: less Consciousness and half the learning, for three days |

### Black Spiral Gifts

Black Spiral Dancers start with Resist Pain, Resist Toxin, Sense Wyrm and Shroud. Some also have:

| Gift | What it does |
|---|---|
| Bane Protector | A bane fights beside them |
| Ears of the Bat | Finds anyone hiding within 20 cells and ends their hiding |
| A Thousand Voices | Mortals within 15 cells who fail Willpower at 6 run. Garou aren't fooled |
| Allies Below | Everyone within 10 cells is knocked down for five seconds unless they make Willpower at 6 |
| Wyrm Hide, Horns of the Impaler | Hide and horns for the fight |

### Possession

A bane's blow on a mortal colonist gets inside unless they make Willpower at 6. Garou, kinfolk and
vampires are safe. The possessed colonist goes psychotic, and a day later becomes a fomor and joins
the Wyrm. The Rite of Cleansing on them, or the Opened Sky's rain, drives the bane out before then.

### The one who got away

A fomor, a First Team mercenary or another of the Wyrm's servants who gets away from a fight with
the colony can come back as its nemesis: renamed, scarred, harder to hurt the way it was hurt, and
with the Wyrm's next raid. A Garou who puts one down earns 3 Glory on top of the kill.

## Raids

- **Fomori raids**, **Black Spiral Dancer raids** (by night, rarer) and **First Team raids** are
  threats like any raid. They take a raid's slot, never add one, and only come to a colony with a
  Garou.
- They come half again as often to a colony with a caern and under the full moon, and a little
  more for each dot of the colony Garou's Renown.
- About half of the Wyrm's raids on a colony with a caern go for the caern's circle first. See
  [Losing it](Rites-and-the-Caern.md#losing-it).
- Half of them bring one or two banes.
- **A bane-ridden beast**: a bane takes the biggest wild animal on the map. It grows stronger and
  mad, and its death taints the ground.
- The Rite of Caern Building always brings the Wyrm, and lost cubs are sometimes followed.

## Pentex

A **Pentex subsidiary** opens 3 to 8 tiles from a colony with Garou: a plant on tainted ground,
guarded by First Team mercenaries and fomori. While it stands, its runoff seeps into colonies
within 8 tiles every few days, and it sends its First Team now and then.

Send a caravan, clear it, and leave its map with no enemy standing: it closes for good, and each
Garou who was there earns 3 Glory. Septs also ask for this as a mission.

Two rarer sites turn up later in a game, cleared the same way:

| Site | What's there |
|---|---|
| Corrupted factory | Fomori guards and taint 30 cells around. Its waste seeps into colonies within 8 tiles and it sends fomori raids. 4 Glory when it's shut down |
| Black Spiral Hive | The Dancers' den: Black Spiral defenders, four banes, green balefire and taint 35 cells around. It sends Black Spiral raids. 6 Glory when it's broken |

### Pentex fronts

Now and then a trader caravan that looks like any trading faction's sells plain goods at half price
or less. It's Pentex. Each hour its traders stay, they taint a small patch where they stand. Sense
Wyrm sees through them, and then they drop the act and attack.
