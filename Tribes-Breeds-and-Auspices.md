# Tribes, breeds and auspices

Three things make a Garou who they are: the tribe they belong to, the breed they were born as,
and the moon they were born under. Each gives a list of Gifts the Garou learns cheaply. See
[Gifts](Gifts.md).

## Tribes

Thirteen tribes can be played. The tribe sets the Garou's starting Willpower and names its
totem. It also changes how the Litany weighs on them and how easily they fall into Harano.

- The **Black Furies** are nearly all women: a man is born to the tribe only as a Metis.
- The **Red Talons** take no homids.
- The **Children of Gaia** count a beaten foe's surrender and the weak twice as dear, and the
  **Get of Fenris** care little for surrender. See [the Litany](Frenzy-and-the-Litany.md#the-litany).
- The **Children of Gaia** and the **Fianna** fall into [Harano](Frenzy-and-the-Litany.md#harano)
  half again as deep.

A fourteenth tribe, the Black Spiral Dancers, belongs to the Wyrm and is never the player's. See
[The Wyrm](The-Wyrm.md).

| Tribe | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Black Furies** | Defenders of the wild places, and of women and children who have nowhere else to turn. Nearly all of them are women. A man is born to the tribe only as a Metis. |
| **Bone Gnawers** | Scavengers who live on the edges of cities, among the poor and the forgotten. Other tribes look down on them. They survive anything, and they eat anything. |
| **Children of Gaia** | Peacemakers and healers. They hold that the Garou's worst wounds are the ones they give each other, and they show mercy where other tribes would not. |
| **Fianna** | Bards and revellers who love a song, a drink and a fight in about equal measure. They keep the old tales alive, and some say the fey still walk with them. |
| **Get of Fenris** | Warriors who measure everything by strength. They don't forgive weakness in others or in themselves, and they meet the Wyrm head on. |
| **Glass Walkers** | The Garou of cities and machines. They believe the fight has to be won where people live, and they put technology to work for it. |
| **Red Talons** | Wolves who want humanity gone and the world given back to the wild. They take no human-born Garou, and their kin run on four legs. |
| **Shadow Lords** | Ambitious and ruthless politicians who believe the Garou need a strong hand, and that the hand should be theirs. |
| **Silent Striders** | Wanderers and messengers who never stay long in one place. They travel faster than anyone and carry word between septs, and the dead follow them. |
| **Silver Fangs** | The royal line, which has led the Garou for as long as anyone remembers. Proud and noble, and prone to madness from breeding too long within their own line. |
| **Stargazers** | Seekers who look for balance through discipline and patient thought. They keep their Rage on a shorter leash than most Garou. |
| **Uktena** | Keepers of hidden lore. They study the mysteries other Garou avoid, and they watch over things that were bound away long ago and must stay bound. |
| **Wendigo** | Fierce guardians of the cold northern wilds. They are hard as the winter they live in, and slow to trust the tribes who came from across the sea. |
| **Black Spiral Dancers** | Garou who walked the Black Spiral Labyrinth and came out serving the Wyrm. Mad, twisted and many of them Metis, they breed in hives under poisoned ground and hate the other tribes more than anything alive. |

### Tribal grudges

Some tribes have old quarrels. Two Garou of feuding tribes think 10 less of each other, halved
when they're packmates. Kinfolk don't hold grudges. A sept whose tribe has a
grudge with your colony's starts out liking you less.

| Tribe | Feuds with |
|---|---|
| Silver Fangs | Shadow Lords |
| Red Talons | Glass Walkers, Bone Gnawers |
| Wendigo, Uktena | Black Furies, Fianna, Get of Fenris, Shadow Lords, Silver Fangs |
| Get of Fenris | Children of Gaia, Black Furies |

## Breeds

| Breed | Born as | Breed form | Starting Gnosis | Primal-Urge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Homid | Human | Homid | 1 | 1 |
| Metis | The child of two Garou | Crinos | 3 | 1 |
| Lupus | A wolf | Lupus | 5 | 2 |

- A Homid in Homid form and a Lupus in Lupus form heal as a mortal does and take silver as a
  mortal does. In any other form they regenerate, and silver burns.
- A Metis regenerates in every form, and silver burns them in every form. Metis turn up only as
  lost cubs or in a scenario.
- Primal-Urge is rolled with Stamina for a [shift](Forms-and-Shifting.md#shifting).

| Breed | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Homid** | Born human and raised among people, with no idea what they were until the First Change. Homids handle tools, trade and talk well, but the spirit world comes to them slowly. |
| **Metis** | The child of two Garou, which the Litany forbids. Born in the war form, always marked by some deformity, and unable to have children. Raised inside a sept, a Metis knows Garou ways better than anyone, and many Garou still look down on them. |
| **Lupus** | Born a wolf, and a wolf at heart in any shape. Lupus are at home in the wild and the spirit world, and have a hard time with tools and polite talk. |

## Auspices

The moon on the night of the First Change sets the auspice. A starting Garou can pick one in the
making menu, or take tonight's moon.

| Auspice | Moon | Role | Starting Rage | Renown its elders want most |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ragabash | New | Trickster, questioner | 1 | Any |
| Theurge | Crescent | Seer, spirit-talker | 2 | Wisdom |
| Philodox | Half | Judge, keeper of the Litany | 3 | Honor |
| Galliard | Gibbous | Moon-dancer, lorekeeper | 4 | Glory |
| Ahroun | Full | Warrior | 5 | Glory |

- Each Garou fills their Rage to the top under their own auspice's moon.
- A deed on the auspice's own Renown track counts one more and gives back a point of Willpower.
  See [Renown, rank and experience](Renown-and-Rank.md).
- Theurges start with two rites, the rest with one, and learn rites in half the lessons.
  Theurges and Philodox have one more Rituals dot. See [Rites and the caern](Rites-and-the-Caern.md).
- A Theurge within 10 cells can speak to spirits for a Garou who can't.

| Auspice | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Ragabash** | Born under the new moon. The trickster and the questioner, who tests the rules so the rest remember why they have them. |
| **Theurge** | Born under the crescent moon. The seer, who speaks with spirits and knows the rites. |
| **Philodox** | Born under the half moon. The judge, who keeps the Litany and settles disputes between Garou. |
| **Galliard** | Born under the gibbous moon. The moon-dancer, who keeps the tales and sings the pack into battle. |
| **Ahroun** | Born under the full moon. The warrior, first into every fight against the Wyrm. |
