# Frenzy, the Litany and Harano

Garou have no Humanity to lose. What holds them back is the Litany and what other Garou think of
them. What breaks them is frenzy, the Thrall of the Wyrm and Harano.

## Frenzy

When something provokes a Garou, they roll their current Rage against a difficulty set by the
moon. Four or more successes and the wolf takes over.

| Moon | New | Crescent | Half | Gibbous | Full |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Difficulty | 8 | 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 |

Rank 3 adds one to the difficulty, ranks 4 and 5 add two. The Merit Calm Heart adds two, the
Flaw Short Fuse takes two off, and the setting "Frenzy difficulty" moves every roll.

**What provokes a roll:**

- a wound, once a fight: the first wound after an hour without one;
- an insult;
- starving, or being caged, about once an hour;
- a duel for the pack's lead;
- with Kindred loaded, a vampire drinking from them or feeding in front of them.

**Holding it off.** With **Resist frenzy** on (on the form bar, on by default), a point of
Willpower stops it. Nothing stops the Thrall.

**Berserk.** The Garou takes the war form and attacks: strangers first, kinfolk and packmates
last. A Garou whose permanent Rage is higher than their Gnosis goes for packmates too. It lasts
at least an hour, as a vampire's frenzy does, and the player can't give the Garou orders until it
ends. After that it ends once no foe is in sight and a Willpower roll against current Rage
succeeds, tried every few seconds.

**The fox frenzy.** A Garou below half health, or facing three foes or more, runs instead.

Drafted Garou frenzy too; you get a message, not a choice. A frenzy costs a point of Wisdom, and
hurting the colony's own Garou or kin in one costs three more.

The **Rage running high** alert lists the Garou with enough Rage, under tonight's moon, that the
next wound or insult could set off a frenzy. Keep them away from trouble or let them rest.

## The Thrall of the Wyrm

Six or more successes on a frenzy roll is the Thrall, but only for a Garou who carries Wyrm taint
or broke the Litany this season. A clean Garou never falls to it. The Flaw Short Fuse makes it
always possible.

In the Thrall the Garou attacks anyone, packmates included, and no Willpower holds it off. It
ends as a frenzy does, costs four Wisdom, and leaves Wyrm taint behind, deeper each time: faint,
marked, one step from the spiral, lost. A letter warns you one step before the end. At the last
step the Garou is lost for good: they have danced the Black Spiral and join the Wyrm's servants.

The Rite of Cleansing washes taint out.

## The Litany

The Garou's code. Breaking a tenet costs Honor, and often Wisdom, shown over the Garou with a
message. Breaking it in front of other Garou of the colony costs half again as much. A tribe may
weigh a tenet more or less.

A Garou who broke a tenet this season can be the subject of a
[punishment rite](Rites-and-the-Caern.md#punishment-rites), and can fall to the Thrall.

**What breaks it in game:**

| Tenet | Broken by | Honor | Wisdom |
|---|---|---|---|
| Garou do not mate with Garou | Two Garou becoming lovers or marrying | 3 | 2 |
| Accept an honourable surrender | Killing a downed person, a prisoner, or an execution | 3 | 1 |
| Do not eat the flesh of humans | Eating human flesh, or a meal made with it | 3 | 2 |
| Keep the Veil closed | Each outsider who leaves remembering the Garou. See [The Veil](The-Veil-and-Septs.md#the-veil) | 2 | 2 |
| Fight the Wyrm wherever it is | With Kindred loaded, striking someone fighting the colony's vampire, once a day | 2 | 0 |

The other tenets are listed for their meaning and kept on trust.

| Tenet | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Garou do not mate with Garou** | Two Garou together make a Metis, born deformed and barren. The Litany forbids it. |
| **Fight the Wyrm wherever it is** | No Garou leaves the Wyrm's servants alone once they know of them. |
| **Respect another's territory** | A sept's land is its own. Hunting or settling there takes leave first. |
| **Accept an honourable surrender** | A beaten foe who yields is spared. Killing a captive is a crime, not a victory. |
| **Submit to those of higher station** | The lower-ranked defer to the higher, and challenge only in turn. |
| **The first share of the kill to the greatest in station** | At a feast, the highest-ranked eats first. |
| **Do not eat the flesh of humans** | People are not prey. A Garou who eats one has taken a step toward the Wyrm. |
| **Respect those beneath you: all are of Gaia** | Kin, captives and animals are Gaia's too. They aren't to be harmed for no reason. |
| **Keep the Veil closed** | Humans must not learn what the Garou are. The last time they did, they hunted the wolves nearly to the end. |
| **Don't let your people carry you** | A Garou who can no longer do their part steps aside rather than be carried. |
| **In peace, the leader may be challenged** | In peace, any Garou may challenge for a higher place. This one permits rather than forbids. |
| **In war, the leader is not challenged** | When the sept fights, its leader is not questioned until the fighting is over. |
| **Never let a caern be violated** | A caern is Gaia's own ground. Losing one through carelessness is among the worst things a Garou can do. |

## Harano

Harano is a grief for the dying world. Any Garou can sink into it.

**What sinks them:**

- a Garou of the colony dying on the same map;
- the Thrall;
- losing the caern.

The Children of Gaia and the Fianna sink half again as deep, and the Flaw Harano Prone doubles
it.

| Stage | What it does |
|---|---|
| Shadowed | A low mood |
| Deep | Worse mood, and the Garou may wander off in sadness |
| Drowning | Worse still, and the Garou may give up and leave the colony |

It eases by itself over weeks. **Gathering for the Departed** eases it faster, by each success.
