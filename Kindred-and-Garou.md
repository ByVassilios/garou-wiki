# Kindred and Garou

With The Rim by Night: Kindred loaded too, vampires and werewolves share a world. Garou call
vampires leeches and count most of them creatures of the Wyrm.

## One kind per pawn

- **Embracing a Garou** is offered, and the confirmation says what happens: the Garou is drained
  to death and never rises. A Garou never asks for the Embrace.
- **Ghouling a Garou** spends the vitae for nothing. No blood bond takes a Garou.
- **Kinfolk** can be ghouled and Embraced. The Embrace ends the kin blood, so a vampire never
  goes through the First Change.

## Garou against Kindred

- **The Wyrm's smell.** A vampire below Humanity 7, or on a Path, smells of the Wyrm. It counts as
  the Wyrm for everything that reads it: Sense Wyrm, Fangs of Judgment, the Gifts that only touch
  the Wyrm, the kinfolk's First Change, and double experience for the kill. A vampire with
  Humanity 7 or more reads as "something dead that walks, but not the Wyrm's".
- **Damage.** Garou claws and bites are aggravated to vampires. Fire is aggravated to both.
- **Drinking a Garou.** Each point gives a vampire two vitae, and leaves them on edge: frenzy and
  Rötschreck come harder to resist, and they're quick to pick a fight, until it wears off over a
  few days. The Garou gains three Rage and rolls for frenzy.
- **Minds.** Dominate and Presence work on a Garou, but not one in frenzy or the Thrall, and a
  Garou resists with Willpower plus Rage. Garou Gifts roll against a vampire's own Willpower.
- **The Delirium** doesn't touch vampires, except the Gift Terror of the Dire Wolf.

### Gifts, fetishes and rites that touch vampires

| What | Against a vampire |
|---|---|
| Halo of the Sun | Burns the undead within 18 cells |
| Calm, Calm the Savage Beast | End a vampire's frenzy, at three successes and one |
| Song of Rage | Drives a vampire into its own frenzy |
| Take the True Form | Ends any other shape, a vampire's Protean forms among them |
| Dam the Heartflood | For a few seconds a success, a vampire or ghoul can't spend blood, and a ghoul's blood stops healing them |
| Renew the Cycle | A contest of Gnosis against Willpower. A win is Final Death. Costs a Gnosis a century undead |
| Break the Bonds | Ends blood bonds, Dominate's conditioning, Presence and every other mind bond |
| Fangs of the North, Claws of Frozen Death | The vampire's blood can't close wounds for a day |
| Rending the Craft | Ends Disciplines that are on |
| Thieving Talons of the Magpie | Steals a Discipline |
| The d'siah | Three aggravated levels more |
| Rite of Cleansing, Rite of the Opened Sky | Can't wash out what a vampire is; they leave it in agony for an hour |

## Sharing a colony

A colony can hold both, and it's tense.

- Each Garou thinks less of the colony for each vampire in it, twice that for one that smells of
  the Wyrm. Red Talons, Get of Fenris, Wendigo and Silent Striders mind it half again as much;
  Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers half as much.
- A Garou rolls for frenzy when a colony vampire feeds in sight, once a day at most.
- Vampires within 12 cells of the colony's Garou are uneasy.
- The septs lose 2 goodwill a day while the colony keeps both.
- A Garou who strikes someone fighting the colony's vampire breaks the Litany's "Fight the Wyrm",
  once a day.
- Opinion between a Garou and a vampire can still grow as any colonists' does.

## Enemies of both

- **Hunters** hunt both, armed for every kind the colony holds: silver for Garou, faith and
  training for vampires.
- **The Masquerade and the Veil** are separate scores, kept from one list of witnesses. Someone
  who saw both counts on both.
- **The Black Spiral and Sabbat raid**: a rare raid by night of Black Spiral Dancers with a Sabbat
  vampire for each 400 points of the raid, up to three.

## Each mod's enemies in the other

- **A Garou pack hunt** comes to a colony with vampires and no Garou: a pack of Garou gathers at
  the map edge. A vampire with Humanity 7 or more can **go out to talk them down**, from the
  letter: Charisma plus Social against the pack's best Willpower. They leave, or attack at once.
- **A leech sighting** comes to a Garou colony with no vampire: by night a rogue vampire walks in,
  feeds on a sleeping colonist and leaves. Sense Wyrm finds it while it's there.

These answer to Kindred's attack setting, and the combined raid to "The Wyrm attacks" as well.

### Feuds

Each side names the colony's pawn who hurt its own, for two quadrums or until that pawn dies:

- A colony vampire who feeds on or kills kinfolk is named by the nearest sept. While they're on a
  home map, the pack hunt comes three times as often, even to a colony with Garou, and its letter
  names them.
- A colony Garou who destroys a court's vampire, or the childe of one of its members, is named by
  that court. Its sect's raids come as often as in a blood hunt, and its stalkers strike at the
  named Garou first.

In the background, septs and courts within 30 tiles of each other fight now and then: a sept's
raid destroys one of the court's vampires, never its ruler, or the court's ghouls strike the caern
for a level. Both chronicles say so.
