# Character sheet

What's on a Garou besides their tribe, breed and auspice: Attributes, Merits and Flaws, and two
Backgrounds, Totem and Ancestors.

## Attributes

The nine Attributes of the tabletop game, on the Garou tab's Attributes page: Strength,
Dexterity, Stamina, Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance, Perception, Intelligence and Wits.

They're read from the colonist as they are: skills, health and traits. Becoming a Garou doesn't
change them. The forms do, while the Garou is in them; see
[Forms and shifting](Forms-and-Shifting.md#the-five-forms). Crinos can take Strength to nine.

Each rank past Cliath adds a tenth to what an Attribute dot gives, Stamina aside. Gifts and rites
roll Attributes too: Seek Gnosis rolls Wits, shifting rolls Stamina.

## Merits and Flaws

A starting Garou picks a Merit and a Flaw in the making menu, or none. A Garou made any other
way, at the First Change or as an outsider, rolls: usually nothing, sometimes a Merit or a Flaw,
now and then one of each. They're never bought. They show on the Character page; click one to
read it here.

Some pairs can't go together: Calm Heart and Short Fuse, Iron Will and Weak-Willed, Acute Sense
and Bad Sight, Long-Distance Runner and Lame, Immune to Wyrm Emanations and Taint of Corruption.

### Merits

| Merit | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Acute Sense** | Sharp-eyed even for a Garou. Sees better than anyone around them. |
| **Long-Distance Runner** | Can run fast and keep running. Moves quicker than others, in every form. |
| **Huge Size** | Close to seven feet tall in human form, and huge in the others. Takes a beating better than most. |
| **Metamorph** | Changes shape as easily as breathing. Every shift works, with no roll. |
| **Calm Heart** | Stays calm when others wouldn't. Frenzy is two harder to fall into. |
| **Iron Will** | Hard to get inside. Three more dice against Gifts and powers that work on the mind. |
| **Natural Channel** | Slips through the Gauntlet more easily than most. Stepping sideways is one easier. |
| **Immune to Wyrm Emanations** | Gaia guards this one against the Wyrm's poisons. Toxins and toxic air do far less. |
| **Silver Tolerance** | Silver still leaves wounds that heal slowly, but it hits no harder than steel. |

### Flaws

| Flaw | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Hard of Hearing** | Misses sounds others catch. |
| **Bad Sight** | Poor eyesight that nothing fixes, and regeneration doesn't touch it. |
| **Lame** | Old damage to the legs that never healed right. Walks slowly, with a heavy limp. |
| **Short Fuse** | Rage burns hotter in this one. Frenzy is two easier to fall into, and the Thrall of the Wyrm can take them at any time. |
| **Weak-Willed** | Easily pushed around. Never resists a Gift that works on the mind, and Willpower never goes above 4. |
| **Pierced Veil** | Mortals see this Crinos for what it is and remember it. No Delirium, so every witness costs the Veil. |
| **Harano Prone** | Falls into dark moods easily. Every loss sinks twice as deep into Harano. |
| **Taint of Corruption** | The Wyrm touched this one long ago, and the mark stays. Sense Wyrm finds them, and no rite washes it off. |

## Totem

Each Garou's standing with the totem spirits, from 0 to 5. A pack pools its members' dots to win
a totem's favor: the dots together must reach the totem's cost. A Garou starts with 1 or 2 dots
and buys more on the Pack page at 2 experience a dot. See [Packs and kinfolk](Packs-and-Kinfolk.md).

## Ancestors

How readily the Garou's ancestor spirits answer, from 0 to 5. A Garou starts with 0 to 2 dots and
buys more on the Character page at 2 experience a dot.

Once a season, **Call the ancestors** rolls these dots at difficulty 8. Pick a skill: each success
lends four levels of it for four hours. With no dots, the button doesn't show.
