# Renown, rank and experience

Garou society runs on Renown, and Renown sets rank. Experience pays for Gifts and Backgrounds.

## Renown

Three tracks, each with temporary points (0 to 10, in brackets on the Garou tab) and permanent
dots (0 to 10).

| Track | For |
|---|---|
| Glory | Courage and victory |
| Honor | Duty, keeping one's word, keeping the Litany |
| Wisdom | Insight, dealings with spirits, restraint |

Deeds give temporary Renown, shown over the Garou as it happens. Ten temporary points in a track
become a permanent dot through the Rite of Accomplishment, or by winning an elder's
[rank challenge](The-Veil-and-Septs.md#rank-challenges) at a sept.

A gain on the auspice's own track counts one more and gives back a point of Willpower: Glory for
Ahroun and Galliards, Honor for Philodox, Wisdom for Theurges. Ragabash have none.

## Rank

| Rank | Name |
|---|---|
| 0 | Cub |
| 1 | Cliath |
| 2 | Fostern |
| 3 | Adren |
| 4 | Athro |
| 5 | Elder |

A cub becomes a Cliath through the Rite of Passage. Each later rank needs the auspice's permanent
Renown, then a rite or a challenge to win it. Hover the rank on the Garou tab to see what the
next one needs.

| Rank | Ragabash (any mix) | Theurge | Philodox | Galliard | Ahroun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cliath | 3 | Wisdom 3 | Honor 3 | Glory 2, Wisdom 1 | Glory 2, Honor 1 |
| Fostern | 7 | Glory 1, Wisdom 5 | Glory 1, Honor 4, Wisdom 1 | Glory 4, Wisdom 2 | Glory 4, Honor 1, Wisdom 1 |
| Adren | 13 | Glory 2, Honor 1, Wisdom 7 | Glory 2, Honor 6, Wisdom 2 | Glory 4, Honor 2, Wisdom 4 | Glory 6, Honor 3, Wisdom 1 |
| Athro | 19 | Glory 4, Honor 2, Wisdom 9 | Glory 3, Honor 8, Wisdom 4 | Glory 7, Honor 2, Wisdom 6 | Glory 9, Honor 4, Wisdom 2 |
| Elder | 25 | Glory 4, Honor 9, Wisdom 10 | Glory 4, Honor 10, Wisdom 9 | Glory 9, Honor 5, Wisdom 9 | Glory 10, Honor 9, Wisdom 4 |

Rank matters for:

- which Gifts the Garou can learn (no higher than their rank);
- frenzy, which is harder from rank 3;
- strength: each rank past Cliath adds a tenth to what an Attribute dot gives (Stamina aside);
- skill: every Garou has Melee and Shooting of at least 6, 9, 12, 15 or 17 from Cliath to Elder;
- Gifts: a Garou made above Cliath, the player's or another faction's, starts with a Gift for each
  rank past Cliath, of that rank where its lists have one;
- the pack's alpha and the sept's offices.

## Experience

A Garou earns experience:

- a little each day;
- a little for each point of Renown earned;
- for each enemy killed, by how dangerous it was, twice that for a creature of the Wyrm.

The setting "Experience gained" scales all of it. Experience pays for:

| What | Cost |
|---|---|
| A Gift of the Garou's own breed, auspice or tribe | Its rank x 3 |
| Any other Gift | Its rank x 5 |
| A Totem dot | 2 |
| An Ancestors dot | 2 |
| A point of permanent Rage | Its current rating |
| A point of permanent Gnosis | Its current rating x 2 |
| A point of permanent Willpower | Its current rating |

Rage, Gnosis and Willpower are bought on the Character page, up to 10.

Rites cost nothing: they're taught. A Gift needs a teacher too. See [Gifts](Gifts.md#learning-a-gift).
