# Being Garou

A Garou is a werewolf: born of Garou blood, Changed for the first time as a young adult, and
then never fully human again. This page covers what that means for a colonist.

## The moon

One lunar cycle runs each quadrum, read from the date.

| Day of quadrum | Moon | Its auspice |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2, 15 | New | Ragabash |
| 3, 13, 14 | Crescent | Theurge |
| 4, 11, 12 | Half | Philodox |
| 5, 6, 10 | Gibbous | Galliard |
| 7, 8, 9 | Full | Ahroun |

The moon rises at 18:00 and sets at 6:00. The first time each night a Garou is outdoors under
the moon, they gain Rage by its phase: 1 at the new moon, 2 at the crescent, 3 at the half and
gibbous, 4 at the full. Under their own auspice's moon they fill to the top. Underground, or on a
map with no sky, the phase still counts, but no one gains Rage at moonrise.

The moon also sets how easily a Garou frenzies (hardest at the new moon, easiest at the full),
and the Wyrm raids half again as often under the full moon.

## Rage

Rage is the wolf's fury, from 1 to 10. The bar under the Garou's name shows what's there now
against the permanent rating.

**Gaining it:**

- moonrise, as above;
- half a point for each wound an enemy deals;
- a quarter of a point when a packmate within 15 cells is hurt;
- half a point an hour within 8 cells of [Wyrm taint](The-Wyrm.md#wyrm-taint);
- three points when a vampire drinks from them (with Kindred loaded).

At rest, an hour after the last harm, Rage sinks back toward the auspice's starting Rage over a
day.

**Spending it:**

- an instant shift to any form, for 1 Rage ([Forms and shifting](Forms-and-Shifting.md));
- **Fury**, an ability every Garou has: 1 Rage, and for four hours the Garou strikes in melee a
  quarter faster;
- staying on their feet past the point where they'd go down, a point every three seconds, as long
  as Rage lasts. Not with no legs, nor when knocked out cold. A downed Garou who gets Rage back
  stands up again;
- the Gifts that cost Rage.

High Rage is what makes frenzy likely. See [Frenzy, the Litany and Harano](Frenzy-and-the-Litany.md).

## The Curse

Mortals feel the predator in a Garou. A mortal colonist who isn't kinfolk is uneasy within 12 cells
of a colony Garou whose permanent Rage is above their Willpower: -2 mood when it's 1 or 2 points
above, -4 at 3 or 4, -6 past that. Kinfolk, psychopaths and bloodlust pawns don't mind.

- A Garou's social impact falls 5% for each point of Rage above 3, and in Homid their trade prices
  are 1% worse per point of Rage. Kin get the same penalty as anyone else.
- Taming and training go at 0.8 the chance, 0.6 above Rage 6. Not in Lupus, nor for a Garou who
  knows Beast Speech. Tame animals don't turn on anyone.

The setting "The Curse" scales all of it, and at 0% turns it off.

## Gnosis

Gnosis is the Garou's tie to the spirit world, from 1 to 10. It powers Gifts and fetishes, and a
Garou needs it to [step sideways](The-Umbra-and-Spirits.md#stepping-sideways).

**Getting it back:**

- **Seek Gnosis**: a button on the Garou. They meditate where they stand, rolling Wits and
  Enigmas at difficulty 8. Each success brings back a point of Gnosis for each hour spent. Once a
  day, and one harder for each further day that week.
- standing near the colony's [caern](Rites-and-the-Caern.md#the-caern), which gives a quarter
  of a point a level every so often;
- a spirit's favor, paid for with [chiminage](The-Umbra-and-Spirits.md#talking-and-chiminage);
- the Reawakening, a Gnosis caern's Opened Caern rite, and a few Gifts and rites.

## Willpower

Willpower is set by the tribe. It holds off frenzy, makes a roll succeed, and pays for some
Gifts. A Garou gets a point back for a deed on their auspice's own Renown track, all of it for a
quest done, and a point at a time from their pack's shared pool when they run below 2.

How a Garou spends it is set on the form bar: **Resist frenzy** spends a point to hold off a
frenzy when Rage boils over.

## Regeneration

Garou regenerate. Every second, the worst wound that isn't aggravated loses 4 HP, and each HP
healed costs nutrition. A starving Garou doesn't regenerate.

- A Homid in Homid form and a Lupus in Lupus form heal as a mortal does. In any other form they
  regenerate.
- A Metis regenerates in every form.
- **Aggravated wounds** don't regenerate: silver, fire, and the claws and fangs of supernatural
  things. They heal at a mortal's pace.

A regenerating Garou shows it with a green-gold glow and sparks, even when it's down and can't move,
so you can tell a Crinos lying in the dirt is mending.
- **Lost parts** grow back: in a day, or in three days for a part lost to silver or claws.

The setting "Regeneration" scales how fast it goes.

## Silver

Silver is a Garou's bane. A blade or blow of silver deals aggravated damage to a Garou, and hits
half again as hard. Anything made of silver counts, modded items included.

A Homid in Homid form or a Lupus in Lupus form takes silver as a mortal does. A Metis is burned
by it in every form. The Merit Silver Tolerance takes the extra harm away, though the wound is
still aggravated. The setting "Silver damage" scales the extra harm.

## Hunger

Garou eat half again as much as a mortal in Homid and Glabro, twice as much in Crinos and Hispo,
and a fifth more in Lupus. Raw food doesn't bother them. They won't eat human flesh unless
ordered to, and doing it breaks the Litany.

## The Delirium

A mortal who sees a Garou in Crinos within 15 cells is overcome by a primal terror, checked once
per mortal. How they react depends on their Willpower:

| Willpower | Reaction | Remembers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collapses, catatonic, for about an hour | No |
| 2 | Flees the map in a panic | No |
| 3 | Hides until it passes | No |
| 4 | Attacks with whatever is in hand | No |
| 5 | Flees, shutting doors behind them | No |
| 6 | Drops their weapon and begs | No |
| 7 | Fights or flees as a soldier would | Explains it away |
| 8 | Keeps watching, curious | Explains it away |
| 9 | Fights on, in a bloodlust | Yes |
| 10 | No reaction at all | Yes |

Kinfolk, other supernatural beings, mechanoids and animals don't suffer it. A mortal's
Willpower is the same each time, raised by the Nerves trait and by True Faith. The few who keep
their wits are a danger to the [Veil](The-Veil-and-Septs.md#the-veil).
