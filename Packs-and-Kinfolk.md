# Packs and kinfolk

## Packs

A pack is two or more Garou bound to one totem spirit.

### Forming one

1. Open a Garou's tab, **Pack** page, and press **Form a pack...**.
2. Pick a totem. Each shows the Totem dots it needs.
3. That Garou leads the Rite of the Totem. Every free Garou of the colony on the map gathers for
   it, and those in the circle when it ends form the pack.

Their Totem dots together must reach the totem's cost. A Garou can also **Join a pack...** that
already exists, or **Leave the pack**.

### What a pack gets

- **Rage** when a packmate within 15 cells is hurt.
- **A better mood** within 10 cells of each other.
- **The totem's gifts**, while the Ban holds: stat changes for every member, Renown once on
  joining, and for some totems a shared Willpower pool, Rage each lunar cycle, faster learning or
  changed Renown awards.
- **Shared Willpower**, for totems that have it: a member below 2 Willpower draws a point at a
  time. The pool refills every lunar cycle.
- **A shared fight**: packmates go for the same enemy.
- **Stepping sideways together**: packmates within 6 cells cross on the same roll.
- **A chronicle** on the Pack page: who joined, left, died, broke the Ban.

### The Ban

Each totem asks something of its pack. The game checks these:

| Totem | Ban |
|---|---|
| Falcon | No member may lose a dot of Honor |
| Boar | No member may kill a wild boar |
| Griffin | No Homid may join |
| Rat | No member may kill vermin, the smallest animals |
| Raven | No member may carry silver or gold |

The rest are shown and kept on trust. A broken Ban takes away every gift of the totem, with a
letter. **Make amends** on the Pack page leads the Rite of Contrition; if it works, the gifts
come back.

### The pack's name

A pack starts with its totem's name. The first deed worth 3 Renown or more in one award earns it a
name of its own from the sept, such as "the Ash Walkers": a line in the chronicle and a point of
Glory for every member. It happens once per pack.

### Totem tasks

At each lunar cycle there's an even chance the totem sets the pack a task, told in a letter and on
the Pack page: slay a Wyrm creature, hunt a wild beast at least the size of a person, take a foe
alive, tend someone, lead a rite, pay chiminage or sense the Wyrm. The packmate who does it gains 2
Renown for the deed, and the pack is in the totem's favour, with a full Willpower pool, until the
cycle ends. A task left undone lapses at no cost.

### The alpha

The pack's leader is whoever won the last challenge, or else the highest rank, then the most
Renown. In peacetime, a packmate of the alpha's rank or higher can **Challenge the alpha**: the
alpha picks a duel or a staring match, and the better roll wins. A Garou who frenzies in a duel
loses it and a point of Honor. Packmates start challenges on their own now and then.

No challenges while enemies are on the map.

Now and then a Get of Fenris or Silver Fang Ahroun of the Nation, of the colony's strongest Garou's
rank or one higher, comes to test them. The contest is the alpha challenge's. A win is 2 Glory and
a loss costs 1. Turning the challenger away costs 2. It only happens while the Nation isn't hostile.

## Kinfolk

Kinfolk are humans and wolves of Garou blood who never Changed. They know what the Garou are, the
Delirium doesn't touch them, and a frenzied Garou goes for them last. Select one and open the
**Kinfolk** tab.

Each has a tribe and a blood: weak, true or strong.

### Where they come from

- Blood relatives of a Garou are kin half the time, at creation and later.
- Kinfolk of a tribe come to join a colony that has a Garou, and so do wolves of the tribe.
- The Kinfolk family and pack scenarios start with them.

The setting "Kinfolk events" turns the joiners and lost cubs off.

### The homestead

When the Free Rangers are placed, the colony's kinfolk get a settlement of their own, a friendly
faction 3 to 6 tiles from home. It trades as a sept does, and kin join the colony twice as often.

Now and then the village calls for help: a Pentex outpost 1 to 3 tiles from it. Clear it, and the
village thinks better of the colony. Let the offer run out, or accept and let the 15 days run out,
and the village is destroyed. It never comes back, and every Garou and kin colonist takes it hard.

### Standing with the pack

**Join a pack...** on the Kinfolk tab lets kin stand with a pack. They share its company and its
chronicle, but not its totem's gifts.

## The First Change

Some kinfolk could still Change. Nobody knows which until a colony Garou has spent three days
within 12 cells of them; then the Kinfolk tab says whether the wolf could still wake.

For one who could, four moments can force it, each only once:

- being close to death;
- facing a creature of the Wyrm;
- seeing a loved one killed;
- a caern rite performed within 20 cells.

The chance each time is 10% for weak blood, 25% for true, 50% for strong. The setting "Kinfolk
who Change" scales it. The Kinfolk tab lists which moments are still open.

The Change is violent. The kin tears into Crinos, frenzies for a short while, and the Delirium
hits anyone in sight who doesn't know. They become a Garou of their tribe, their auspice set by
that day's moon, a cub of rank 0 with one breed Gift. A wolf that Changes becomes a Lupus Garou.
A cub needs the Rite of Passage to become a Cliath.

## Lost cubs

Now and then a letter tells of a young Garou alone after a First Change they don't understand:
Homid most of the time, sometimes Lupus, rarely Metis. **Take them in**, and they arrive at the
map edge as a cub, half the time with hunters or the Wyrm on their heels. Taking one in pleases
the Free Rangers. **Turn them away**, and they're gone.

A colony with no Garou at all can get a **stray cub** instead, more rarely: the same youth, but
nobody there knows what they are, so the letter only speaks of a hurt stranger at the edge of
your land with something following them. Taken in, they're the colony's first Garou. The stray
cub has its own slider on the Event chances page.
