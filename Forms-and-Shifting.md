# Forms and shifting

Every Garou has five forms. The one they were born in is their breed form: Homid for a Homid,
Lupus for a Lupus, Crinos for a Metis.

## The five forms

| Form | Strength | Dexterity | Stamina | Manipulation | What it's like |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Homid | +0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | The human form. Anything can be worn or held, and nobody looks twice |
| Glabro | +2 | +0 | +2 | -2 | A near-man brute. Clothes and armour still fit, just. Mortals see an ugly man and nothing worse |
| Crinos | +4 | +1 | +3 | -3 | The war form. Claws and bite deal aggravated wounds. A fifth faster. Mortals who see it suffer the [Delirium](Being-Garou.md#the-delirium) |
| Hispo | +3 | +2 | +3 | -3 | A dire wolf as big as a horse. Half again as fast, a crushing aggravated bite, sharper sight and hearing, no hands |
| Lupus | +1 | +2 | +2 | -3 | A wolf. Twice as fast as a man, the keenest senses of any form. Outsiders see only a wolf |

The Attribute changes show on the Garou tab's Attributes page while the Garou is in the form.
Crinos, Hispo and Lupus also change how much a Garou eats; see
[Being Garou](Being-Garou.md#hunger).

## Gear

| Form | What stays |
|---|---|
| Homid | Everything |
| Glabro | Everything |
| Crinos | Melee weapons. Clothes, armour and guns come off |
| Hispo, Lupus | Nothing worn or held |

By default, what a form can't hold drops at the Garou's feet, as at the table. With the setting
"Gear stays with the Garou" on, it goes with them and comes back when they change back.

Hispo and Lupus have no hands, so the Garou can't do work that needs them.

## Shifting

Select the Garou and use the **form bar**: five buttons, one per form. Hover one to see how the
change would go.

- **Back to the breed form** is always instant and free.
- **Slowly, by roll**: Stamina plus Primal-Urge at difficulty 6. Each form passed through on the
  way needs a success and takes two seconds. A failed roll means the change doesn't happen.
- **At once, for 1 Rage**: straight to any form.

The Merit Metamorph makes every shift succeed as if with five successes. Some Gifts hold a body
in its shape, and then no shift works.

## In fights

The form bar has two switches.

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| In fights | When a fight starts, or when drafted with enemies on the map, the Garou changes form for a point of Rage, picked as below. Drafting with no enemies on the map changes nothing. An hour after the fight they go back to the resting form. On by default |
| Resist frenzy | Spend a point of Willpower to hold off a frenzy when Rage boils over. It does nothing against the Thrall of the Wyrm. On by default |

The fight form is the first of these that fits:

1. Lupus, at double speed, when the Garou is running away.
2. Glabro with a gun in hand, since Glabro can still hold one.
3. Glabro in sight of outsiders, to spare the Veil, until below 60% health.
4. Hispo to run down a target more than 15 cells off or fleeing, back to Crinos within 5.
5. Out of Crinos while holding back a frenzy: on "Resist frenzy", with the Rage to frenzy, and the
   moon not their auspice's. Crinos makes frenzy easier.
6. Crinos against a mortal who suffers the Delirium.
7. The auspice's lean (Ahroun Crinos, Ragabash Glabro), then the tribe's (Red Talons and Silent
   Striders Hispo), then Hispo for a Lupus, then Crinos.

Hispo and Lupus only come with empty hands. Allied Garou of other factions cast their Gifts once
they have a target or are hit, and once hit they fight with the tactics of an enemy pack.

A Garou in a frenzy takes the war form on their own. See
[Frenzy, the Litany and Harano](Frenzy-and-the-Litany.md).

## Being seen

An outsider who sees a Crinos may remember it, and so may one who sees a Garou change into any
form, or a Hispo walking about. Each who leaves the map remembering hurts the Veil. See
[The Veil, septs and hunters](The-Veil-and-Septs.md#the-veil).
