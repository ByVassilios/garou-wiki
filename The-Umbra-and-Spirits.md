# The Umbra and spirits

Beside the world lies the Umbra, the spirit world. The part closest to the physical world is the
Penumbra, and a Garou can walk there.

## The Penumbra

The Penumbra is a map of its own, a mirror of the home map. It has the home map's ground, rock
and trees, full grown, but nothing the colony built. Where the colony built, a faint Weaver's
web covers the ground, brighter where a building takes power, and it slows whoever walks through
it. The caern and places of power shine. Old ruins stand solid, and so do masterwork and
legendary art. Wild plants glow, and small lights drift up from plants, webs and blight. The home
map's Wyrm taint shows as blight, and banes come up out of it.

The nights are moonlit: the darkest at the new moon, almost day at the full.

- **Wyld glades**: one for every 400 wild plants at home, 3 at most. Each is a lush circle with
  spirit fruit, a point of Gnosis each, and sometimes a Curiosus.
- **Lingering trees**: a stump at home stands here as a faded tree, 12 at most. **Lay it to rest**
  for a point of Wisdom, and the nearest nature spirit counts it as chiminage.
- **Shadows of suffering**: graves, corpses, prisoners and colonists in a mental break at home each
  leave a shadow. Every four of them bring up a bane, three at most.
- **Something new each trip**: each Penumbra holds one of a lost spirit whose first lesson is free,
  a nest of two banes on blight, or a talen lying on the ground. A message says which.

The map is made the first time someone steps sideways, and thrown away when none of the colony is
left in it. A large home map gets a smaller Penumbra, 250 cells wide at most.

Anyone who dies in the Penumbra stays there, with everything they carry.

## The Gauntlet

The wall between the worlds. Its strength is the difficulty of crossing it, set by how much the
colony has built within 15 cells.

| Around the Garou | Gauntlet |
|---|---|
| At most 2 colony buildings | 5 |
| Up to 15 | 6 |
| Up to 60 | 7 |
| More | 8 |
| Near a caern | 5 less the caern's level, never under 2 |

## Stepping sideways

Select a Garou and press **Step sideways**. They stare into a reflection and roll Gnosis against
the Gauntlet.

- **A reflection:** still water next to the Garou, or a pocket mirror in the inventory at one
  harder. The pocket mirror is made of steel at a crafting spot or machining table. Mirrorshades
  take two off.
- **The roll:** one success takes five minutes, two thirty seconds, three crosses at once.
- **Failure:** the Gauntlet won't give for an hour.
- **A botch:** the Garou is caught in the Gauntlet, stuck between the worlds for an hour.
- **Packmates** within 6 cells cross on the same roll, with their gear.
- The Merit Natural Channel makes it one easier.

**Step back** is the same roll from anywhere in the Penumbra, with no reflection needed.

### Getting lost

A Garou with no Gnosis can't cross either way, except at the caern or a **thin spot**. Each
Penumbra map has three thin spots, glowing, out where the land is wild, with a Gauntlet of 3. A
Garou can cross there within 3 cells.

Thin spots are springs of Gnosis too. A Garou within 3 cells of one gets back a quarter of their
Gnosis every so often, and within 8 cells of the shining caern half, whatever its level.

### Peeking across

**Peek across** rolls Gnosis against the Gauntlet. For an hour the Garou sees the home map's people
and animals as grey shadows, hostile ones in red.

### Pulled across

Once an hour, a colony Garou within 4 cells of Wyrm taint or a bane on the home map has a small
chance of being dragged into the Penumbra, with a letter. Getting back is the usual roll.

## Spirits

| Kind | Where they come from |
|---|---|
| Nature spirits | One for every 40 trees on the home map, up to 5 |
| Pattern spiders | Weaver spirits, one for every 30 colony buildings, up to 3 |
| Lunes | Moon spirits: one, or two at the full moon |
| Animal spirits | One for every 8 wild animals on the home map, up to 3, each named for one of them |
| Elementals | Earth for a lot of natural rock, water for a lot of water, fire for a geyser or a fire, glass for six or more colony lights, electricity for a generator |
| Banes | The Wyrm's spirits, always one or two. They hunt whoever of the colony comes within 12 cells |
| Curiosi | Puzzle spirits, sometimes in a glade |
| Englings | One at the full moon, the prey of the Sacred Hunt |

No more than 12 at once. Spirits don't eat, sleep, bleed or leave a body.

**Banes** come in kinds. Each acts at most once an hour:

| Bane | What it does |
|---|---|
| Scrag | Provokes a frenzy in a Garou within 8 cells |
| Drattosi | Attacks other spirits within 15 cells |
| Psychomachia | May cross over and ride an animal at home |

Killing a bane in the Penumbra lifts the blight within 6 cells. Blight lifted in the Penumbra, by a
rite, a Gift or a kill, lifts the Wyrm taint at the same place at home.

**Curiosi** set a puzzle: **Solve its puzzle** for a point of Gnosis, Perception plus Enigmas at 9.
Each success gives a point of Gnosis and one of Willpower.

**Englings** come at the full moon. Killing one is the Sacred Hunt: every Garou within 20 cells
gets their Gnosis back in full.

### Fading

A spirit beaten down doesn't die. It slumbers for six hours, then fades away. Until then a Garou
can **drain** it for a full Gnosis pool; that costs a point of Honor unless it's a bane. The Rite of
the Fetish can bind any slumbering spirit.

### Talking and chiminage

Right-click a spirit with a Garou who has the Gift Spirit Speech, or with a Theurge within 10
cells, and pick **Talk to ...**. Spirits won't help for nothing: each asks its chiminage, an
offering carried in the inventory (wood or berries, silver or gold, steel or components, kibble
or pemmican) or, sometimes, a bane driven off. Each kind has two offerings, and a spirit takes
whichever of them the Garou carries. The Step sideways tooltip and the spirit's grimoire page list
them. Once it's paid, ask it to:

- **teach a Gift** whose teacher is its kind (lunes teach rites too);
- **give Gnosis**, filling the Garou's Gnosis;
- after a lesson and Gnosis, **a favor** of its kind: nature spirits cleanse blight, water spirits
  heal wounds, animal spirits send their kin home, elementals cross over as an ally for a day, and
  Weaver spirits repair the colony's buildings.

Paying chiminage is a promise kept: a point of Wisdom. Then the spirit asks again for the next
favor. A paid spirit is also willing to go into a fetish or talen.

**Caern tradition**: a Gift asked for away from a caern of the Gift's level or higher costs double.

**Moods**: an animal spirit is offended once the colony has taken 30 meat or more from its kind,
and a nature spirit by 10 or more stumps. Either way its price doubles. A Weaver spirit is pleased
by 150 or more buildings and halves its price.

The **Rite of Summoning**, led in the Penumbra, calls a spirit of the kind you pick.

### Manifesting

Strong spirits cross over now and then. Half of the Wyrm's raids bring one or two banes in their
own shape; they come apart after half a day. At a moot, the caern's totem shows itself and stays
three hours beside the ritemaster.

## Pathstones

About one Penumbra map in ten holds a pathstone, guarded by a spirit. It's what the Rite of the
Opened Bridge needs. See [Rites and the caern](Rites-and-the-Caern.md#the-opened-bridge).
