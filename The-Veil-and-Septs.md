# The Veil, septs and hunters

## The Veil

The Litany forbids letting humans learn what the Garou are. The Delirium hides most of what they
see. The danger is the few who keep their wits.

### Witnesses

An outsider becomes a witness when they see a colony Garou:

- in Crinos, and have the Willpower to keep their wits (see [the Delirium](Being-Garou.md#the-delirium));
- change shape into any form;
- walk about as a Hispo, on the colony's map or in a settlement;
- work a Gift no mortal could explain (one worked in Crinos adds nothing);
- or see a body a Garou tore open with claws or teeth, until it's buried, burned or rotted away.

A message names each witness. At Willpower 9 or more they remember; below that they explain it
away. Kinfolk, fomori and the colony's own don't count.

**Dealing with them.** Kill, imprison or recruit a witness before they leave the map, or make
them forget. The "Witnesses on the map" alert lists everyone left.

### The score

Each witness who leaves the map adds to the Veil score: 10 for one who remembers, 3 for one who
explained it away, twice that for a trader. It fades by 1 a day.

A Garou seen by a mortal who will remember loses 5 Honor, once a day at most, and breaks the
Litany for each such witness who gets away.

| Level | Score | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet | under 20 | Nobody outside is talking about wolves |
| Rumours | 20 | Travellers tell stories. Hunters start visiting as travellers, carrying silver |
| Hunted | 50 | The stories reached hunters. Bands of them come by day |
| Exposed | 80 | The hunting order sends everything it has. The septs think 30 less of you |

A letter marks each level. The Sept tab shows the score. The setting "The Veil" turns witnesses
and the hunters they bring off.

## Hunters

A hunting order, the hunters of The Rim by Night: Core.

- **The raid** comes by day once the Veil is Hunted, a probing band below Exposed and the whole
  order at Exposed. The band pulls back at nightfall. It also needs Core's hunter setting.
- **Silver.** Every hunter's melee weapon is remade in silver.
- **Finishing.** A hunter goes for a downed Garou first and kills them where they lie.
- **Visits.** From Rumours on, hunters come as travellers now and then, more often as the Veil
  falls.

## The septs' faction

The septs trade as an ordinary-looking faction whose name, color, people and post labels change every game, so its name tells you nothing.

The first time the colony has a Garou, the septs appear: three septs 6 to 20 tiles away,
with a letter. It's a faction like any other, though it never raids.

- **Each sept** leans to a tribe and keeps a caern of a type and level, shown on the world map.
  A sept whose tribe has a grudge with yours starts out liking you less.
- **Trade.** Septs sell plain goods and a few fetishes and talens, and buy them back. Their
  caravans and visitors come as any faction's do.
- **Missions.** Once the Nation knows you, it asks for help: shut down a Pentex plant, or kill the
  banes a sept's Theurges saw coming, which cross into your map a few hours after you accept. Each
  done is worth 15 goodwill; one let lapse after 15 days costs 7. Taking in a lost cub is worth 15
  too.
- **Help.** When the Wyrm raids and the Nation is your ally, half the time a pack of three sept
  Garou arrives to fight beside you. With an Opened Bridge, they come at once.
- **Moon bridges.** Gifts that open moon bridges reach twice as far to a sept or caern.

The Nation goes on without you, and the Sept tab keeps its news. Elders are challenged, cubs go
through the First Change, and packs go after the Wyrm and don't come back. A caern can grow
stronger or lose a level to banes, and a caern that loses its last level falls: the sept is gone
and Pentex builds on the ground. Pentex also opens plants near septs, and each one costs the
nearest sept a caern level every season until someone shuts it down. You get a letter when that
happens, and shutting the plant down earns 10 goodwill with the Nation on top of the Glory. While
the Nation is short of the septs it started with, it founds a new one about once a year. With
Kindred loaded, septs fight the vampire courts near them, and with Imbued, hunter cells hunt them.
A sept no rival can reach may be struck by the Wyrm instead.

### When a sept falls

A sept's elder and notables have names, the rest are a headcount, and they outlive its fall. Some
die, some go missing, and the rest go to another sept, their own tribe first, or found a new one.
Kinfolk go to the nearest sept. The same happens when the sept's settlement is razed, by you or by
another mod.

- **The Veil.** Each sept has a rating, shown in words on its inspect pane once you know it. Pentex
  sites nearby, wars and your own Garou breaching the Veil wear it down; time and rites mend it. A
  sept with a thinner Veil is attacked more often.
- **Sieges.** When a war is about to break a sept you know, it is under siege for a few days first,
  and a quest lets you go and help. Who dies on the map is dead in the world.
- **Letters.** A sept you know falling gets a letter: who did it, if anyone knows, a few of the
  dead, who is missing. Taking a sept yourself says so. Galliards at moots bring rumors of the rest.
- **Refugees.** Survivors who like the colony and are within reach may ask to join. With a caern,
  survivors of a nearby sept may ask to make it their sept.
- **Vengeance.** A sept lost to a war, a taken town or the Wyrm puts your Garou under an oath for 30
  days. Until it's settled they are unhappy about it, the sept's own tribe more so. The first of the
  foe one of them kills settles it, with Glory for everyone in the fight and a good mood for every
  Garou in the colony. Losing a second sept to the same foe renews the oath.

Core's settings, under The world of darkness, have a switch for all of this (off: a fallen home's people just vanish), a bloodiness slider for falls with no battle, and a world pace slider.

### Rank challenges

A caravan at a friendly sept has a **Rank challenge** button. The sept's elders set a challenge
for one of its Garou who has a full ten temporary Renown or enough for the next rank: Willpower
plus rank against 7. Winning does what the Rite of Accomplishment does. Losing costs 1 Honor, a
botch 3.

## The colony as a sept

Once the colony has a caern, it's a sept. The **Sept** tab, on the bar at the bottom, shows the
Veil and one row per office. Each office takes one colony Garou of rank 1 or more, and one Garou
holds one office. A year in office is worth Glory, Honor and Wisdom.

| Office | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Sept leader | A stronger moot for everyone, and 1 Honor for the leader at each moot |
| Master of the Rite | A die more leading a rite on a map with a caern |
| Warder | 1 Rage an hour while enemies are on the caern's map |
| Guardian | Sight and hearing up a tenth |
| Den parent | The colony's cubs and kinfolk on the map learn a quarter faster |
| Talesinger | Each Garou's deed worth 2 Renown or more is worth 1 more |
| Truthcatcher | A title only |

Without a caern the colony is a pack, not a sept, and the offices stay empty.
