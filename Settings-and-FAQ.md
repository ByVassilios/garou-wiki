# Settings and FAQ

## Mod settings

Options, Mod settings, The Rim by Night: Garou. Four pages: Rules, Rites and spirits, World and
events, and Event chances, where each Garou event has its own slider from never to three times as
often. The search box at the bottom finds a setting on any page by its name or description.
**Reset to defaults** sits at the bottom too, and with a game loaded so does **Remove Garou from
this game**.

A setting you have changed shows its name tinted with a reset arrow before it, each tab counts its
changed settings, and **Changed only** by the search box lists just those.

**Rules: Garou**

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Experience gained | Scales all experience a Garou earns, 25% to 300% | 100% |
| Renown gained | Scales the Renown earned for deeds, and so how soon a Garou rises in rank, 25% to 300%. Renown lost for breaking the Litany stays as it is | 100% |
| Rage gained | Scales the Rage from the moon, wounds, insults and the rest, 0% to 300%. At 0% a Garou only has the Rage they start the night with | 100% |
| Regeneration | Scales how fast wounds close on their own, 0% to 300%. Aggravated wounds still won't regenerate | 100% |
| Silver damage | Scales the extra harm silver does, 50% to 200%. At 100% it hits half again as hard as steel | 100% |
| Frenzy difficulty | Added to every frenzy roll, -3 to +3. Higher means fewer frenzies | As in W20 |
| Kinfolk who Change | The chance a kinfolk with the blood goes through the First Change, from thin blood to strong. Shown as the lowest and highest chance; the highest goes up to 100% | 10% to 50% |
| The Curse | Scales how much a Garou's Rage unsettles mortals, 0% to 200%. At 0% the Curse is off. See [Being Garou](Being-Garou.md#the-curse) | 100% |
| Gear stays with the Garou | What a form can't wear or hold goes with the Garou and comes back when they change back. Off, it drops at their feet | Off |

**Rules: Systems**

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Frenzy | Off, wounds, insults and hunger never send a Garou into frenzy or the Thrall of the Wyrm. A Gift that calls up a frenzy still does, and so does the First Change | On |
| Harano | Off, losses no longer sink a Garou into Harano. Harano they already have fades as usual | On |
| The Litany | Off, breaking a tenet costs no Honor or Wisdom and doesn't count against the Garou this season | On |
| The Delirium | Off, no one panics or flees at a Crinos, and colonists aren't shaken. Outsiders remember what they saw, as with the Pierced Veil flaw, so the Veil suffers more | On |
| Challenges for alpha | Off, packmates never challenge the alpha on their own | On |
| Wounds roll for frenzy once a fight | Only the first wound of a fight, after an hour without one, rolls for frenzy. Off, every wound does | On |
| The Thrall needs taint | Only a tainted Garou, one who broke the Litany this season, or one with Short Fuse can fall to the Thrall. Off, any Garou can, as in W20 | On |
| Black Fury men are born Metis | Off, a man can be a Homid or Lupus Black Fury too | On |

**Rites and spirits**

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Garou to build a caern | The circle the Rite of Caern Building needs, 1 to 6 | 3 |
| Gnosis to build a caern | The Gnosis the circle pours in, 0 to 30. Each Garou still gives up a permanent point | 10 |
| Totem tasks | The chance each lunar cycle that a pack's totem asks a task, 0% to 100% | 50% |
| A misused spirit stays silent | Days a fetish's spirit won't answer after it is misused, 0 to 10 | 3 days |
| A Gnosis point wakes a fetish | Off, every use of a fetish is a Gnosis roll | On |
| Moon bridges on the world map | Off, the Gifts that jump across the world map are greyed out | On |

**World and events**

Switching one off stops it from happening again. Anything already under way plays out.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| The Wyrm attacks | Fomori, Black Spiral Dancers, Pentex First Teams and banes come for colonies with Garou, and Pentex opens plants nearby | On |
| The Veil | Outsiders who keep their wits after seeing a Garou spread the word, and hunters come | On |
| Veil damage | Scales how much the Veil score rises for each outsider who leaves with what they saw, 0% to 300% | 100% |
| Veil healing | Scales how fast the Veil score falls back each day, 0% to 300% | 100% |
| Kinfolk events | Kinfolk and wolves of the tribe come to join, and lost and stray cubs turn up | On |
| Towns for each sept | One Garou Nation sept for this many ordinary towns, 3 to 30. Lower means more septs. Takes effect on a new world | 10 |

The Core settings, under The Rim by Night: Core, hold "Grimoire" and "Power tiles", the hunter
setting the Veil's hunters need, and "Nemeses": enemies who get away come back with their next
raid. Its World and rules page also holds when the first stray cub can come, cover names for the
monster factions, lupus ignoring moods a wolf wouldn't feel, and prisoners keeping their secret.

## FAQ

**Can I add this to an existing save?**
Yes. Nothing changes until someone becomes Garou. The septs appear the first time the
colony has a Garou.

**Can I remove it from a save?**
Yes, with the button made for it. Load the game, open the mod settings and press **Remove Garou
from this game**. Everyone on the Penumbra comes home first. Then every Garou becomes mortal
again, kinfolk lose their blood, and the mod's items, buildings, spirits, factions, quests and
sites are deleted, the septs and the homestead with them. The game is saved under a new name and
taken back to the main menu. Turn the mod off and load that save. Removing the mod without doing
this first leaves the save broken. With Kindred still loaded, Core stays and keeps what Kindred
needs.

**My colony has no Garou. How do I get one?**
A kinfolk colonist can go through the First Change (see
[Packs and Kinfolk](Packs-and-Kinfolk.md#the-first-change)). Without kin, wait for a stray cub: a
rare offer in the Quests tab about a hurt, half-wild stranger at the edge of your land. Accept it and they're
the colony's first Garou, and kin and lost cubs start coming after that.

**Do I need any DLC?**
No. It runs on Core alone.

**Does it work with Kindred?**
Yes. See [Kindred and Garou](Kindred-and-Garou.md).

**Does it work with modded races?**
The Garou tab and silver are added to every humanlike race at startup, and nothing assumes a
human body layout. A form changes stats and looks, never the body.

**Does it work with Combat Extended?**
Yes, through The Rim by Night: Core. With CE loaded, the claws and bites, the fetish weapons,
the Gifts' armour, projectiles and damage are all in CE's units.

**Will it slow my game down?**
It shouldn't. Pawns who aren't Garou cost nothing per tick, and nothing scans every pawn on the
map.

**My Garou keeps frenzying.**
Watch the moon: the frenzy roll is at 4 under the full moon and 8 under the new. Keep high-Rage Garou out of
fights and away from insults, keep them fed, and leave **Resist frenzy** on. Calm Heart helps.
The setting "Frenzy difficulty" moves every roll.

**My Garou won't heal.**
Silver, fire and supernatural claws leave aggravated wounds, which don't regenerate. A starving
Garou doesn't regenerate either. A Homid in Homid form heals as a mortal does: change forms.

**My Garou went over to the Wyrm.**
They fell to the Thrall too often. Each time leaves deeper taint, and a letter warns you one step
before the end. Clean taint with the Rite of Cleansing, and keep tainted Garou and Litany breakers
calm.

**My Garou can't step back from the Penumbra.**
They're out of Gnosis. Walk them to a thin spot, the glowing places out in the wild, or the caern.

**Hunters keep showing up.**
The Veil is falling. Deal with witnesses before they leave, keep Crinos out of sight of
outsiders, and the score fades by a point a day.

**Will a town I visit again have the same people?**

Yes. Every town the colony walks into keeps its people between visits, unless they died or left. A friendly town
stays friendly while you're there: its people only turn on you if the faction does.

**Where do I look something up in game?**
The [grimoire](Interface.md#the-grimoire), the book button in the bottom-right corner. Type a
question on its Ask tab.

## Dev mode

With dev mode on, the debug actions menu has a "The Rim by Night: Garou" category. **Make Garou** turns a
colonist into a Garou with rolled defaults, and **Open making menu** lets you choose instead.
Others set the form, give Gifts, rites, Merits and Renown, change rank, provoke a frenzy or the
Thrall, fill or empty Rage, Gnosis and Willpower, bring on moonrise, make kinfolk and set off their First
Change, form a pack or break its Ban, pull a Garou into the Penumbra, place a thin spot, and
**Make mortal**.

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue with:

1. what you did and what happened;
2. your `Player.log` (on Windows, in
   `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Ludeon Studios\RimWorld by Ludeon Studios\`);
3. your mod list.
