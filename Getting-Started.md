# Getting started

## Install

You need RimWorld 1.6, Harmony and The Rim by Night: Core. Load order: Harmony, Core, then
Garou. No DLC is needed. The Rim by Night: Kindred can load alongside it; see
[Kindred and Garou](Kindred-and-Garou.md).

The mod is safe to add to a running save. Your colonists stay mortal until you make one a Garou,
a kinfolk colonist goes through the First Change, or a lost or stray cub turns up.

## Scenarios

Four scenarios start you with Garou.

| Scenario | Who you start with |
|---|---|
| Garou pack | Three Cliath and two kinfolk, already a pack under a totem |
| Lone cub | One Garou of rank 0 who hasn't had the Rite of Passage, and no sept |
| Kinfolk family | Five kinfolk, one of whom has the blood to Change |
| Lost tribe | Three Garou and two kinfolk, all of one tribe, already a pack |

Each colonist in these scenarios starts with one random weapon in hand, picked from the scenario's own list. Some scenarios add a steel helmet, a robe or both. None of the weapons are silver.

To make your own, add the "starting Garou" part in the scenario editor. It sets how many of the
first colonists are Garou, how many after them are kinfolk, whether the Garou are cubs, whether
they start as a pack, and whether everyone is of one tribe.

## The making menu

Each starting Garou gets the making menu, "Who is ...?", before the game begins. A Garou made
with a pawn editor before the game starts gets it too.

| Choice | What it sets |
|---|---|
| Tribe | One of the thirteen. See [Tribes, breeds and auspices](Tribes-Breeds-and-Auspices.md) |
| Breed | Homid, Lupus or Metis. Only the breeds the tribe takes are offered |
| Auspice | One of the five, or "the moon tonight" |
| Breed, auspice and tribe Gift | One rank 1 Gift from each list. A cub gets only the breed's |
| Merit and Flaw | One of each, or none. See [Character sheet](Character-Sheet.md#merits-and-flaws) |
| Fetish | A starting fetish up to level 2 (level 1 for a cub), already attuned |
| Pack totem | Picked by the first Garou of a pack scenario |

Anything left on "(roll for me)" is rolled. **Roll the rest** fills every blank at once. Rank is
always Cliath, or rank 0 for a cub.

When the last menu closes, the kinfolk take the first Garou's tribe and the pack forms. Each
starting Garou gets the Totem dots the chosen totem costs. A totem whose Ban won't take the pack
(Griffin refuses homids) is swapped for one that will.

## The first night

1. Select a Garou and open the **Garou** tab. The Character page shows tribe, breed, auspice,
   tonight's moon, and Rage, Gnosis and Willpower.
2. Look at the form bar above the buttons. "In fights" is on by default, so the Garou goes Crinos
   when a fight starts. See [Forms and shifting](Forms-and-Shifting.md).
3. Find the Garou's Gifts on their tiles, one tile per list. Hover a Gift for its cost and roll.
4. Keep some food around. Regeneration burns nutrition, and a starving Garou doesn't heal.
5. Watch for the "Rage running high" alert, most of all near the full moon. See
   [Frenzy, the Litany and Harano](Frenzy-and-the-Litany.md).
6. Have a look for a place of power: open wild ground with almost nothing built nearby. A caern
   comes later. See [Rites and the caern](Rites-and-the-Caern.md).
