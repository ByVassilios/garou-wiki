# Gifts

Gifts are powers taught by spirits. Each belongs to one or more lists: a breed's, an auspice's
or a tribe's. Each has a rank from 1 to 5, and a Garou can't learn one above their own rank.

## Using a Gift

Select the Garou. Their Gifts sit on tiles above the buttons, one tile per list: breed, auspice,
tribe, and "other Gifts" for the ones learned from someone else's list. Hover a Gift for what it
does, what it costs and what it rolls.

- **Cost:** Rage, Gnosis or Willpower, by the Gift. A Gift with no cost shown is free.
- **The roll:** most Gifts roll a pool against a difficulty, often against the target's
  Willpower. The successes set how strong or long it is. A failed roll still spends the cost.
- **Limits:** no Gifts in a frenzy. Some Gifts need a form, or open sky, or a target asleep; the
  button says why when it can't be used.
- **Always on:** a few Gifts are passive and have no button. Spirit Speech is one.
- **Ending one early:** a Gift that lasts has an **End** button while it runs.
- **From a caravan:** Tech Speak is the one Gift a Garou can use on the world map. Its button is on the
  caravan.
- **On their own:** an undrafted Garou who is set to attack, or has just been hurt, uses Gifts
  with a fighting use against enemies nearby, as Kindred do with Disciplines.

Right-click a Gift's icon to open its page in the grimoire. Click the tile's name for the list's
page.

Some Gifts are seen by outsiders: anything that calls, makes, moves or remakes something. A
witness who keeps their wits counts against [the Veil](The-Veil-and-Septs.md#the-veil). A Gift
worked in Crinos adds nothing; the war form is breach enough.

## Learning a Gift

Open the Garou tab, **Gifts** page, and press **Learn a Gift...**. The list shows the Garou's own
Gifts first. **Gifts of other breeds, auspices and tribes...** opens the rest.

A Gift needs three things:

1. **Rank.** The Gift's rank can't be higher than the Garou's.
2. **Experience.** The Gift's rank times three from the Garou's own breed, auspice or tribe,
   times five from any other list. Some Gifts need another Gift first.
3. **A teacher.** One of these:

| Teacher | How it works |
|---|---|
| A spirit, called | "Call a ... spirit to teach it": a point of Gnosis and about an hour and a half of meditation, then Gnosis against 5 plus the Gift's rank. A failure spends the Gnosis and the Gift stays unlearned |
| A higher-ranked Garou | "Learn it from ...": five lessons, one a day, taken during research work |
| A spirit in the Penumbra | Talk to a spirit of the Gift's kind and pay its chiminage. See [The Umbra and spirits](The-Umbra-and-Spirits.md#talking-and-chiminage) |

The Gift's page says which kind of spirit teaches it. Learning a Gift is worth a point of Wisdom,
and teaching one is worth Honor and Wisdom. See [Renown, rank and experience](Renown-and-Rank.md).

## Starting Gifts

A new Cliath knows one rank 1 Gift from each of their breed, auspice and tribe. A cub knows only
the breed's, and gets the other two at the Rite of Passage.
